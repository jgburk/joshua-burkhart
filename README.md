# Joshua Burkhart

## Bio

I am a trained biomedical informatician, scientist, and software architect based in Honolulu, HI. I hold an Affiliate Assistant Professorship at the University of Hawaii John A. Burns School of Medicine, where I focus on systems medicine and networks research. Before that, I worked as a postdoctoral researcher in the U. Hawaii Bioinformatics core. My software development career included positions in enterprise IT, automotive, and defense industries, engineering UX, back-end, and embedded products. I hold a Ph.D. in Biomedical Informatics from Oregon Health & Science University, where my dissertation applied machine learning to improve Reactome tissue specificity, and I am now completing an MBA at the U. Hawaii Shidler College of Business.

This repository is my portfolio: each engagement here is organized by the capability it demonstrates, with the brief, the analysis, and the decision memo that prove it.

## Layout

- `capabilities/` — one folder per demonstrated capability

- `docs/decisions/` — the recommendation, written after each engagement
- `data/` — sourced inputs with provenance
- `analysis/` — notebooks, scripts, and figures
- `AGENTS.md` — AI working conventions (canonical)
- `prompt-log.md` — record of AI sessions that mattered
- `RESUME.md` — resume
