# Perfect competition — engagement brief

One or more of the crops must be planted less (6 fewer beds overall). If it's decided poorly, we'll lose money at the market and might not be able to keep our farm. The choice is limited by our budget (20K), labor hours, and a few other variables. Overall, this appears to be a multivariate revenue optimization problem but I'll just use my intuition for this assignment (stage 1). In a professional setting I'd work to put together the equations and compare algebraic solutions to other numerical approaches to optimization, like the Nelder-Mead method.

I assume there's no crop failure or loss for any reason, that all the business processes are 100% efficient, and there are no taxes of any sort.
